Optimal blend (bbl/day)

This problem has more than one optimal blend: different blends reach the same maximum profit. The solver returns one of them, so your blend can differ from another solution while the profit is the same.

Constraints

A binding constraint is at its limit. The value column is the shadow price (dual value): the change in daily profit from relaxing that limit by one unit, other things fixed. It holds for small changes; solve again to price a large one. A constraint with slack is worth $0. A binding constraint can also be worth $0 when another constraint would stop profit from rising.
