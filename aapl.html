14 judgment calls made without review. Each is flagged where it was made; open to see them all.
  • Line items Other non-current assets. Treated as operating, so the model invests about 20 cents in this line for every dollar of sales growth. The alternative is 'excluded' (on the view that the deferred tax and right-of-use pieces, $32B, are not cash investments), which would raise the value by about $4.30 a share, because the line is 20 percent of sales. A line cannot be split, and the cash flow statement shows real cash going into it every year.
  • Line items Other current liabilities. Treated as operating, with the year-1 ratio set low enough to pay the $8.8B transition-tax instalment through working capital. The alternative is to carve the $8.8B out as a debt claim in the bridge; the value effect is about the same (the payment falls in year 1 either way), and this keeps the line whole.

Everything downstream inherits this, so it is worth disagreeing with here rather than later.

Sales growth

History 2020 5.5%, 2021 33.3%, 2022 7.8%, 2023 -2.8%, 2024 2.0%, 2025 6.4%  ·  Suggested 14.5%, 6.0%, 5.0%, 4.5%, 4.0%, then 3.5% forever  ·  Moving it 1pp each way moves the value 32.1%

Year 1 is fiscal 2026, which ends in September 2026 and is three-quarters reported: December +16%, March +17%, June +16%, with September guided to +9% to +11%. Adding the reported nine months ($364.4B) to the guided midpoint for September (about $112.7B) gives about $477B, or 14.5% growth, so year 1 is close to a fact rather than a forecast. The years after that are the real assumption. Fiscal 2026 was an unusually strong iPhone cycle (iPhone 17, iPhone Air, MacBook Neo) on top of double-digit Services growth; the five years before it averaged about 8% including the 33% pandemic year, and 2% to 6% outside it. Management gives no guidance beyond the current quarter. A constant percent fade from the guided year toward the terminal rate is the right method. Argue for a higher path if you believe Apple Intelligence drives a multi-year upgrade cycle and Services keeps compounding in the teens; argue for a lower path, even a decline in year 2, if fiscal 2026 pulled demand forward or memory and advanced-node supply constraints cap units. Terminal growth is a perpetual nominal rate.

EBITDA margin

History 2020 28.2%, 2021 32.9%, 2022 33.1%, 2023 32.8%, 2024 34.4%, 2025 34.8%  ·  Suggested 35.5%, 35.0%, 35.0%, 35.0%, 35.0%, then 35.0% forever  ·  Moving it 1pp each way moves the value 6.2%

Apple reports no EBITDA, so the history is operating income plus depreciation and amortization from the cash flow statement. It is after stock-based compensation ($12.9B in fiscal 2025), which is a real cost and stays in. The margin rose from 32.9% in 2021-2023 to 34.4% in 2024 and 34.8% in 2025, driven by mix: Services carries a gross margin near 76% against about 37-40% for Products, and Services grew from 17% to 26% of sales over six years. The first nine months of fiscal 2026 ran at 36.3%, but that includes tariff refunds (about 2 points of gross margin in the June quarter and 1 point guided for September) that will not recur. Against that, R&D is growing much faster than sales (up 32% in the June quarter) and memory prices are rising sharply. A constant percent of sales is the right method. Argue for a higher margin if Services mix keeps rising and R&D growth slows; argue for a lower one if memory costs persist and Apple absorbs them rather than raising prices.

Depreciation / prior net PP&E

History 2021 30.7%, 2022 28.2%, 2023 27.4%, 2024 26.2%, 2025 25.6%  ·  Suggested 26.5%, 26.5%, 26.5%, 26.5%, 26.5%, then 26.5% forever  ·  Moving it 1pp each way moves the value 0.7%

Judgment call Total D&A ($11.7B) used, not PP&E depreciation alone ($8.0B). The alternative, a rate near 17.5%, would lower capex by about $3.7B a year and raise taxable EBIT, and would raise the value by about $4.10 a share, but it assumes the amortizing non-PP&E assets are never replaced.

Depreciation here is the cash flow statement's 'depreciation and amortization' ($11.7B in fiscal 2025) divided by prior-year net PP&E. It fell from 30.7% in 2021 to 25.6% in 2025. Only $8.0B of that is depreciation of PP&E; the other $3.7B is amortization of assets carried in other non-current assets. That is why realized capex from the PP&E roll-forward ($15.9B in 2025) runs $3-4B above cash PP&E capex ($12.7B). Using the full D&A keeps EBIT equal to reported operating income and makes the capex plug pay for replacing those amortizing assets too. Nine-month fiscal 2026 D&A of $10.0B annualizes to about $13.3B, or about 27% of the opening PP&E. Hold it constant; argue for a lower rate if you think the amortizing non-PP&E assets are not replaced.

“Depreciation expense on property, plant and equipment was $8.0 billion, $8.2 billion and $8.5 billion during 2025, 2024 and 2023, respectively.”10-K, 10k/AAPL_10-K_FY2025_filed2025-10-31.htm, Note 5, Property, Plant and Equipment

Why the suggestion sits where it does. 26.5% is between the 2025 rate (25.6%) and the rate implied by fiscal 2026 D&A to date (about 27%).

Cash tax rate on taxable income

The driver is a marginal cash rate on EBIT. Apple's effective rate was 14.7% in 2023 and 15.6% in 2025; 2024's 24.1% includes the one-time $10.2B State Aid charge.     The model

    Sales grow at g. EBITDA is a margin on sales. Net PP&E at the end of year t is next year's sales divided by the sales-to-PP&E turnover — the one line that leads sales by a year, because capacity is built ahead of the volume it serves. Depreciation is a rate on prior-year net PP&E, and capex is the plug that reconciles the two; in steady state it lands at (g + d) times prior net PP&E. Every other operating asset and liability is a ratio to the same year's sales. Free cash flow is EBITDA less cash taxes on EBIT, less capex, less the change in working capital. Taxes are unlevered; the interest shield lives in the discount rate. A running NOL carryforward shelters the explicit years; anything still alive after year 6 is valued separately and appears in the bridge, so the perpetuity is taxed at the full rate and stays a genuine steady state.
